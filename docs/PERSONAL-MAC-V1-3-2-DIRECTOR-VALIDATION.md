## Comparison target

Compare:

1. **V1.3 bad output** — orientation/contract and editorial failures.
2. **V1.3.1 portrait-but-barren output** — correct engineering and QA, conservative plan.
3. **V1.3.2 enriched output** — source-derived portrait format plus bounded Director enrichment.

## 1. Fresh project and transcript

1. Start with `npm run dev:product`.
2. Create a new project; do not reuse prior review state.
3. Ingest the same Short.
4. Confirm effective source dimensions and 1080×1920 portrait output.
5. Transcribe with `ggml-medium.bin`.
6. Confirm canonical IDs, timing, Bengali text integrity, and provenance.

## 2. Initial Director diagnostics

Run the live `qwen3:30b` Director and retain:

- initial analysis;
- initial Director-quality summary;
- Editorial Opportunities;
- provider/model provenance;
- canonical coverage;
- initial meaningful recommendation count;
- initial static risk and visual variety score.

Confirm coverage and editorial activity are shown separately.

## 3. Enrichment behavior

If the initial plan is LOW-ACTIVITY:

1. Confirm exactly one enrichment request.
2. Confirm only eligible weak canonical segments were requested.
3. Confirm prayer/Scripture/altar-call/emotional sections were excluded.
4. Confirm no canonical ID or timing changed.
5. Confirm the final status is ENRICHED or remains transparently LOW-ACTIVITY.
6. Restart/retry and confirm the successful enrichment cache prevents another request.

If the initial plan is already NORMAL, confirm enrichment is NOT NEEDED.

## 4. Section-level visual planning

Inspect the same Short for:

- hook/main point: concise sermon point, caption, punch-in, or reframe;
- question: emphasis caption or reframe;
- teaching: at least one restrained visual variation when semantically justified;
- story: contextual B-roll consideration or an explicit alternate treatment;
- prayer: calm speaker-led treatment without activity inflation.

No exact timestamp or quota is required.

## 5. Caption and text quality

For each caption/graphic:

- compare it with the canonical transcript;
- confirm it is concise;
- confirm it is not the entire transcript section;
- confirm it does not fabricate theology or corrected Scripture;
- confirm Bengali remains readable;
- approve display text explicitly.

## 6. Story/B-roll resolution

If the Director recommends B-roll:

- inspect the search concept and explanation;
- choose only a usable owned/approved asset;
- otherwise choose Keep Pastor, reject, reframe, punch-in, or concise caption;
- confirm no fake asset or unrelated visual appears.

## 7. Review Workspace diagnostics

Record:

- canonical coverage;
- editorial activity;
- Director quality;
- meaningful edits;
- no-change duration;
- untreated story count;
- visual variety;
- enrichment status/attempt count.

Investigate any remaining long eligible static stretch before rendering.

## 8. Render and realization

Render the bounded 60-second project.

Require:

- 1080×1920 portrait;
- preserved subject framing;
- zero dropped approved operations;
- zero unsupported approved operations;
- every approved reframe, punch-in, caption, graphic, and B-roll treatment visible;
- calm prayer presentation.

## 9. QA comparison

Run unchanged engineering and Editorial Quality QA.

Compare V1.3.1 with V1.3.2:

- meaningful edit count;
- longest eligible static stretch;
- visual variety;
- story treatment;
- no-change duration;
- B-roll realization;
- caption/graphic quality;
- viewer comprehension and attention.

Do not lower the QA gate to obtain a pass.

## 10. Human judgment

Watch all three versions completely and record:

- Is V1.3.2 visibly more intentional than V1.3.1?
- Does it remain reverent?
- Are normal teaching/story sections less barren?
- Are captions and graphics concise and faithful?
- Is story B-roll contextual?
- Is prayer still calm?
- Does the edit avoid constant-cut short-form styling?

V1.3.2 may be declared editorial GO only after the live enriched result, unchanged QA, realization audit, and human comparison all pass.
